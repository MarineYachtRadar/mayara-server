By brand

Navico Simrad, Lowrance, B&G — BR24, 3G, 4G, HALO. Any subnet, wired only. Furuno DRS-NXT, DRS/X-Class, FAR series, DRS4W. Needs a 172.31.x.x address. Raymarine Quantum, Cyclone, RD series, Magnum. Needs DHCP on the radar network. See also Quantum networking. Garmin GMR HD, xHD, xHD2, xHD3, Fantom, Fantom Pro. Needs a wired 172.16.x.x address. Koden MDS-R series via RADARpc control boxes. Untested — reports welcome.

Reaching the radar picture

Mayara belongs on a machine wired to the radar network. Everything you look at afterwards — the built-in web interface, a Signal K server, a chart plotter app — talks to Mayara over ordinary HTTP and WebSocket, and works fine over WiFi.
