Navico radar setup

Simrad, Lowrance and B&G radars: BR24, Broadband 3G and 4G, and all HALO models.

What you need

  • The radar and the Mayara machine on the same wired Ethernet network
  • Any IP subnet — Navico radars have no subnet requirement
  • A switch that passes multicast (every unmanaged switch does) or has multicast IGMP support
  • For HALO Doppler: a heading source
WiFi will not work for the radar

A Navico radar pushes roughly 1 MB/s (about 8 Mbit/s) of multicast spoke data. WiFi sends multicast at the base rate — 1 to 6 Mbit/s — and never retransmits it, so the picture arrives full of gaps while the access point's airtime is consumed for everyone else. Wire the radar to Mayara, then view Mayara over WiFi. The full explanation.

Network requirements

Navico radars come up with their own address and announce themselves by multicast, so there is nothing to configure: no DHCP server is required, no static address needs assigning, and the radar does not care what subnet Mayara is on. All that matters is that the radar and Mayara sit on the same Ethernet segment.

Plugging both into the LAN ports of your boat's WiFi router is exactly that, and works fine — a router's LAN ports are a built-in switch, and traffic between them is switched, not routed. What does not work is anything that makes the radar's traffic actually cross a router: the radar on one subnet or VLAN and Mayara on another, the radar behind the router's WAN port, or a VPN or mesh WiFi backhaul between the two. Multicast stops at those boundaries unless someone has deliberately configured it to cross.

Heading and navigation data

HALO radars need heading data before they will enable Doppler (VelocityTrack). Mayara forwards heading, position and speed to the radar automatically once a navigation source is configured: 

# Auto-discover a Signal K server via mDNS
mayara-server

# Or name the Signal K server
mayara-server -n tcp:192.168.1.100:3000

# Or feed NMEA 0183 in
mayara-server --nmea0183 -n udp:0.0.0.0:10110

Without heading data, HALO Doppler mode stays off.

Dual range

4G and most HALO radars can run two independent range windows at once. Mayara presents each as its own radar, suffixed A and B; both appear in the radar list and are controlled separately.

Troubleshooting

Radar not detected

  • Confirm Mayara is on the same wired network as the radar.
  • Confirm the radar has power — the dome or array should be turning, or its status light active.
  • On a machine with several interfaces, name the right one: mayara-server -i eth0
  • On a managed switch, check that IGMP snooping is not filtering the radar's multicast.
  • Run mayara-server -vv and watch for discovery traffic.

Doppler not working on a HALO

  • Check that a heading source is connected — Signal K or NMEA 0183.
  • Confirm heading values actually arrive: they show up in the log with -v.
  • Most HALO firmware supports Doppler, but very old HALO24 firmware may not.
